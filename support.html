FAQ

How do I enter a cheat?
In GTA VI press Up on the D-pad to open the phone, hold L1 + R1 (PS5) or LB + RB (Xbox), then enter the arrow sequence shown in the app.
A cheat doesn't work.
Cheats become available after finishing the main story and meeting each cheat's own unlock condition (see Progress › Cheats in the game). Activating a cheat disables saving and trophies/achievements for that session.
Some cheats have no code yet.
Those codes haven't been published by trusted sources yet. We add them as soon as they are confirmed.
