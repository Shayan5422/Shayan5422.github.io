Codex VI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Codex VI ("the app") is an unofficial, free guide to cheat codes. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app itself stores

Your preferences (language, console, haptics) and favorite cheats are stored only on your device. We do not run servers, do not require an account, and do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device information, and ad interactions. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks for your permission before the advertising identifier can be used. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent through Google's consent message, and can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open a request or see the Support page.
